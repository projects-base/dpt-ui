Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

This policy covers Daily Problem Dynamic Tracker — a single product made of two parts that share one account: the Chrome extension of that name, and this web dashboard. "We", "us" and "our" refer to the developer of that product.

In short: your solutions, notes and generated documents live in your own Google Drive and in your own account on our database. We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use it to train any AI model.

1. Google user data we access

Signing in uses Google OAuth 2.0. We request only the scopes the product actually needs:

Scope What we do with it
userinfo.email Identifies your account. Your email address is the key your problems, notes and settings are stored under, so the extension and this dashboard show the same data.
documents Creates the formatted Google Doc for each solution you log. We only write documents this app creates; we never read your existing documents.
spreadsheets Appends a row to the tracking spreadsheet you choose in Settings, and draws the progress chart in it.
drive.file Creates the "Daily Coding Practice" folder and files the generated Docs into it. This is the per-file scope: it grants access only to files this app itself created, not to the rest of your Drive.

2. Other information we store

  • Your problem log. Titles, links, difficulty, your notes, your self-ratings and the code you choose to save — stored in our PostgreSQL database against your account.
  • Your settings. The spreadsheet URL, Drive folder ID and, if you supply one, your personal Gemini API key.
  • Page context you select. When you use the extension on a problem page, it reads that page title, URL, meta description and the code you highlighted — only when you open the extension, and only on the tab you have active.

3. How we use it

  • To run the product — generating your Docs, updating your Sheet, and rendering your streak and analytics on this dashboard.
  • AI analysis. When you press AI Auto-Fill, the problem title, its link and the code you submitted are sent over HTTPS to the Google Gemini API, which returns a difficulty estimate, complexity analysis and suggestions. Nothing is sent to Gemini unless you press that button.

4. What we never do

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, your code or your notes. We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not use it to train, retrain or improve any generalized AI or machine-learning model. We do not transfer Google user data to third parties except as needed to provide the features described above.

5. Limited Use

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements, and to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy.

6. Service providers

  • Google (Drive, Docs, Sheets, Identity, Gemini) — the integrations above.
  • Neon — hosts the PostgreSQL database holding your account and problem log.
  • Render — hosts the backend API.
  • Netlify — hosts this website.
  • Firebase Realtime Database — used by the extension to mirror your settings between devices, keyed by your email address.

7. Security and retention

All traffic runs over HTTPS. Authentication uses Google OAuth 2.0 — we never see or store your Google password. We keep your data for as long as your account exists, and delete it when you ask us to.

8. Your choices

  • Revoke access at any time from your Google Account permissions page.
  • Delete individual problems from the dashboard at any time.
  • Delete your account and all its data by emailing the address below. We action deletion requests within 30 days.
  • Your Docs and Sheets are yours — they stay in your Drive even if you delete your account here or uninstall the extension.

9. Children

This product is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page, and describe material changes on the sign-in page.

11. Contact

Questions, or a data deletion request: contact us.
